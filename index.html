About Me

Dr. Ning (宁国琛) is currently an Assistant Professor and a Principal Investigator (PI) at Tsinghua University. His research interests encompass structural design and autonomous control of medical robots, with a particular emphasis on intelligence. He also concentrates on research areas involving robot learning and the intersection of robotics and artificial intelligence (AI), with the ultimate goal of achieving autonomous medical robots capable of performing medical tasks in the future.
